This module adds an "End Customer" field to sale orders and customer
invoices.

Some businesses invoice an intermediary party while the actual work is
performed for a different, final recipient. For instance, LyBe Creators
always invoices Blueingenie, but the work itself is carried out for
distinct end customers such as Interval or Terland.

This module lets you record who the real recipient of the work is,
independently of who is being invoiced, on both the sale order and the
customer invoice.
