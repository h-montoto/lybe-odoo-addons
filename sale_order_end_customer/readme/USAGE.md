1. Open a sale order (Sales > Orders) or create a new one.
2. Set the "Cliente Final" (End Customer) field, shown right under the
   "Customer" field, to the partner that is the real recipient of the
   work, regardless of the partner being invoiced.
3. The field is shown as a column in the quotation, sale order and
   customer invoice list views (toggle it from the optional columns
   menu if hidden).
4. In the search bar of quotations, sale orders and customer invoices
   you can search by "Cliente Final" and use "Group By > Cliente Final".
5. The same field is available on customer invoices (Accounting >
   Customers > Invoices), in the "Other Info" tab.
6. Changes to this field are tracked in the record's chatter.
